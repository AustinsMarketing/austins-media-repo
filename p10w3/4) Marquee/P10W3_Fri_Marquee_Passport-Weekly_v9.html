Behind the Marquee
Dani Porter, General Manager

Dani spent the first part of her career in hotels, the kind where the details matter: properties in San Diego, Hawaii and here in Austin, running guest service and group events. Somewhere between the banquets and the breakfast service she figured out that what she cared about most was the food.

She joined us this April. What drew her to Austin’s was the chance to bring that level of hospitality to a place where people come to play, and to raise the bar in the kitchen while she was at it. Six months in, that work has a name: Pizza University, the training every cook goes through before they run the Marra Forni oven, and the Neapolitan pies that came out of it. We think they’re some of the best in Austin. Dani would rather you decide for yourself.

She’s in the building most days. If you see her, say hello.

Mark and Michael, owners
