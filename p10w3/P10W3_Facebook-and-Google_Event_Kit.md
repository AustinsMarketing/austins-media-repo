# Trunk or Treat: Facebook Event and Google Business Event kit

Cover image: P10W3_Event_Cover_Trunk-or-Treat.png (1200 x 628)

## Facebook Event (about two minutes)
1. On the Austin's Facebook Page: Events > Create Event > In person.
2. **Name:** Trunk or Treat at Austin's: FREE on Halloween Day
3. **Start:** Saturday, Oct 31, 2026, 4:00 PM. **End:** 7:00 PM.
4. **Location:** Austin's Park (tag the Page).
5. **Co-hosts:** add every car club and partner Page that joins the car show. Co-hosted events show to each co-host's followers.
6. **Ticket link:** bit.ly/3HTotpm
7. **Description (paste):**

> Candy straight from the trunks of some of Austin's coolest cars. No doorbells. No porch lights.
>
> FREE Trunk or Treat and car show, Saturday Oct 31, 4 to 7 PM.
>
> Come in costume on Halloween Day and receive a $10 Fun Card upon purchase of a Play, Combo or VIP Pass (online or at the counter).
>
> Tickets and details: bit.ly/3HTotpm

8. Upload the cover image. Publish. Then **Invite** all Page followers who like events, and share the event to the Page timeline.

## Rules these follow
Never write the city name Pflugerville. The event is "Austin's Park, right off I-35." Say Fun Card, not game card.
