Behind the Marquee
Dani Porter, General Manager

That’s Dani Porter, our General Manager, with the new Marra Forni. It landed this month, and she has not stopped smiling since.

Dani spent years in hotels in Hawaii, where hospitality isn’t a department, it’s the whole point. Ask her about it and you’ll get the long version. She brought that with her to Austin’s this April, along with a belief that the food at a place where people come to play should be as good as the play.

The oven is where that shows. Every cook who runs it goes through Pizza University first, and the Neapolitan pies that come out of it are, we think, some of the best in Austin. Dani would rather you decide for yourself. She’s in the building most days; say hello.

Mark and Michael, owners

FREE Trunk or Treat
Saturday, October 31 · 4 to 7 PM
Candy straight from the trunks of some of Austin’s coolest cars, courtesy of Sweet Rides of Texas. No doorbells. No porch lights. Just a whole lot of sweet.
Buy your pass
This is how to Trunk or Treat!
Picture this: a lineup of Austin’s coolest cars, trunks popped open and stuffed with candy. Walk the line, say the magic words, fill the bag. No “just one piece.”
1
Show up between 4 and 7 PM. Trunk or Treat is free for everyone.
2
Come in costume (yes, grown-ups too) and buy a Play, Combo or VIP Pass, online or at the counter, to get a free $10 Fun Card.
3
Fill the bag, then play it off: spend the Fun Card in the arcade, then ride, race and bowl into the night.
Every week
•Half-Price Wednesday, all day. 50% off bowling, billiards, Putt Vegas and the video games in the arcade. The only things not half off: ticket-merchandise games and the pusher games.
•Thankful Thursday. First responders, teachers, veterans and active-duty military get a free Play Pass with the purchase of another Play Pass. Valid ID, one per qualifying guest.
•Twilight Friday, 7 PM to close. $34.95 for unlimited attractions after dark: laser tag, go-karts, bumper boats, mini golf, XD Theater, rock wall, the coasters and more.
•Happy Hour, weekdays 4 to 7 PM in Revl. $1 off wells and drafts, $2 off frozen drinks, $4 canned beers, half off Revl games and mocktails.
